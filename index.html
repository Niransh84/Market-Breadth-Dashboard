Method notes

Eligibility. MU in MU-dominant industry or MD in MD-dominant industry. ATR pullback after list assignment: Up 0 ≤ ATRs ≤ 1.5; Down −1.5 ≤ ATRs ≤ 0. CRYPTO out. Rows with missing/zero ATR, close or EMA fields are excluded (never scored 0 ATR).

Scoring. Market: QQQ/LL = ((Equal+Mcap)/2 − 50)×2; ETF = (Equal − 50)×2. Ind Score = signed dominant-quadrant % share (MU +s, SU +s/2, SD −s/2, MD −s). Stock = MU +100 / MD −100.
Trend. Gap % = ema_pct. Gap ATR = (EMA20 − EMA60) / ATR$, with EMA20 = close/(1+price_ema_pct/100), EMA60 = EMA20/(1+ema_pct/100), ATR$ = atrp/100×close. Trend = clamp(Gap ATR × 100/3, −100, +100).
Total = 0.40×Market + 0.30×Industry + 0.15×Stock + 0.15×Trend. Score = |Total|. Total>0 → Up, <0 → Down.

Rank. Score desc → higher |Ind Score| → smaller |ATR to EMA|. Priority ⭐ if |ATR to EMA| ≤ 0.5. Ind Match ✅ = same dominant MU/MD direction on another tab (exact industry name; ETF via approved high/medium map, 37 rows). ▾ = Trend capped (|Gap ATR| ≥ 3).
